# Vegan Milk Kefir

*Pea milk · Transitioned milk kefir grains*

Milk kefir grains cultured on a supplemented pea-milk base: a 5-day dairy taper, then a long-term vegan formula aimed at grain growth with only a trace of alcohol.

## Background

Milk kefir grains are a dairy-adapted SCOBY — bacteria and yeasts bound in a rubbery polysaccharide matrix (kefiran). They can live on a plant base, but an overnight swap from cow’s milk to pea or nut milk often starves the bacteria, lets yeasts run, and shrinks the grains. This protocol tapers reconstituted dairy milk over five days while a pea-protein base plus a mineral buffer takes over.

The maintenance formula is built to keep alcohol down: less honey (yeast fuel), lemon juice as an initial acid shock, red marine algae as a multi-mineral stand-in for dairy, a breathable cover with daytime swirling so yeasts spend energy on cell walls instead of ethanol, an early 12–16 hour harvest, and an immediate fridge lock.

These are **milk kefir grains**, not water kefir (tibicos). Use a stainless-steel mesh strainer and a wooden or stainless spoon. Never rinse the grains.

## Recipe

Flowchart tables (Cooking For Engineers style) live on the [HTML page](vegan-milk-kefir.html). Method below mirrors those tables.

### 1. Initial health evaluation

Before starting, place the grains in a stainless-steel mesh strainer and check them:

- **Size & texture:** mini-cauliflowers — springy, elastic, rubbery
- **Color & aroma:** creamy-white or ivory; cleanly sour or yeast-like
- **Wake-up:** if the grains arrived dehydrated, or spent more than 4 days in transit, run **one** 24-hour cycle in 100% reconstituted dairy milk before Day 1

### 2. The 5-day accelerated transition

Prepare both liquids, then combine them at room temperature (**68°F–74°F**) at that day’s ratio. Total ferment volume is 500 mL. Strain every 24 hours — or earlier if full whey separation occurs.

| Day | NOW dairy milk | Plant base | Harvest |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | 375 mL | 125 mL | 24 h, or earlier if full whey separation |
| 2 | 250 mL | 250 mL | 24 h, or earlier if full whey separation |
| 3 | 125 mL | 375 mL | 24 h, or earlier if full whey separation |
| 4 | 50 mL | 450 mL | Strain **immediately** when pockets of clear whey form |
| 5+ | — | 100% vegan formula | 12–16 h (see below) |

1. **Plant base.** Vigorously blend 4 scoops of Folona pea powder into 500 mL warm filtered water. Stir in 1.5 Tbs. raw clover honey and 1/8 tsp red marine algae powder.
2. **Dairy milk.** Vigorously blend 3/4 cup NOW dry milk powder into 4 cups filtered water.
3. **Combine** the day’s volumes. Introduce the grains.
4. **Strain** with a stainless-steel mesh strainer. Never rinse. Move the grains straight into the next day’s mix.

`[Blend plant base] → [Blend dairy milk] → [Combine day’s ratio] → [24 h at 68–74°F] → [Strain] → [Next day]`

### 3. Long-term vegan batch (day 5+)

1. **The assembly.** Vigorously blend or shake the warm filtered water and 4 scoops of Folona pea powder until completely smooth.
2. **Yeast-suppression buffers.** Stir in the raw clover honey, red marine algae powder, and lemon juice until fully dissolved. Cool the whole mixture to room temperature (**66°F–70°F**) before the grains go in.
3. **Inoculation.** Add the transitioned milk kefir grains.
4. **Oxygenation.** Secure a highly breathable cloth or coffee filter with a tight rubber band. Never use a solid lid. Swirl or stir with a wooden or stainless spoon 2 to 3 times during the day so yeast builds cell walls instead of pumping out ethanol.
5. **Time-controlled harvest.** Do not wait 24 hours. Watch the jar and strain at the **12 to 16-hour mark** — the moment you see the first columns of tiny bubbles and the liquid smells cleanly tart.
6. **Cold lock.** Transfer the strained drink immediately into a sealed jar in the refrigerator. Never rinse the grains; place them directly into the next freshly prepared batch.

`[Blend pea milk] → [Stir honey + algae + lemon; cool] → [Inoculate] → [Cloth cover; swirl 2–3×] → [Strain 12–16 h] → [Fridge the drink; grains to next batch]`

## Best practices

- **No plastic.** Stainless-steel mesh strainer and a wooden or stainless spoon.
- **Never rinse** the grains. Move them straight into the next batch.
- **Never a solid lid** on vegan batches. A cloth or coffee filter keeps oxygen in play.
- **Swirl 2–3×** during the vegan ferment.
- **Harvest early** (12–16 h), not at a 24-hour dairy cadence.
- **Cold-lock** the drink as soon as it is strained.
- **Lemon juice** may be fresh-squeezed or preservative-free bottled — same 1 teaspoon.
- **Wake-up cycle** if grains were dehydrated or in transit more than 4 days: one 24-hour dairy ferment first.

## Functional Benefits

### Live kefir community on a plant base
- **SCOBY:** milk-kefir bacteria and yeasts kept intact by a dairy taper instead of a hard cutover
- **Pea matrix:** Folona pea milk powder (or undiluted soy milk) supplies protein the grains can work
- **Kefiran grains:** the protocol is for grain growth, not a one-off drink

### Low-alcohol controls
- **Less honey** in the maintenance formula limits yeast fuel
- **Lemon acid shock** at inoculation favors a clean tart start
- **Oxygen + swirling** pushes yeast toward growth rather than ethanol
- **12–16 h harvest** and an immediate fridge lock cut the ferment short

### Mineral buffer
- **Red marine algae** stands in for dairy minerals (a multi-mineral buffer at 1/8 tsp per 500 mL)
- Aimed at grain health on a plant substrate, not as a seaweed supplement shot

### How to drink it
- Strain, seal, refrigerate. Drink cold.
- Next batch starts as soon as the grains come out — they should not sit dry
