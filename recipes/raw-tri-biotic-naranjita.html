The RawTri-Biotic Naranjita

Sugar-free · Zero glycemic · Raw bio-active · Artisanal

Orange peels + L. plantarum → inulin & monk fruit ferment → postbiotic syrup → sugar-free chocolate coat

Background

Naranjitas are a classic Chilean candy: orange peels boiled soft, then cooked in sugar syrup until glossy and sweet. This version keeps the chew and bright citrus character, but skips the boil and the sugar — built for health benefits. Thin raw peels ferment with L. plantarum; the reserved tonic becomes a postbiotic syrup with inulin and monk fruit; then a sugar-free chocolate coat. The ferment softens the peels and leaves a pleasant tang that carries through to the finished candy.

Ingredients

Because we are not boiling, slice the peels paper-thin so the bacteria can penetrate the fibers and make them pleasant to chew.

Peels & brine ferment

  • 4–5organic oranges (peels only; scrub thoroughly)
  • 3 cupsroom-temperature filtered water
  • 3 Tbs.inulin powder
  • 1/4 tsp.pure monk fruit powder
  • 1/2 tsp.L. plantarum powder

Optional — more tangy: use up to 3× the inulin and L. plantarum (e.g. 9 Tbs. inulin + 1½ tsp. culture). Keep water and monk fruit as written.

Postbiotic syrup

  • reservedferment tonic (liquid from the brine)
  • 3 Tbs.inulin powder
  • 1/8 tsp.pure monk fruit powder

Optional — more sweetness: use up to 3× the inulin and monk fruit in the syrup (e.g. 9 Tbs. inulin + ⅜ tsp. monk fruit). Pairs well with a tangier ferment.

Chocolate enrobing — choose one

White chocolate

  • 120 gcacao butter
  • 3 Tbs.inulin powder
  • 1/8 tsp.pure monk fruit powder

Dark chocolate

  • 120 gcacao butter
  • 3 Tbs.cacao powder
  • 3 Tbs.inulin powder
  • 1/8 tsp.pure monk fruit powder

Milk chocolate

  • 120 gcacao butter
  • 2 Tbs.cacao powder
  • 2 Tbs.cultured buttermilk powder
  • 3 Tbs.inulin powder
  • 1/8 tsp.pure monk fruit powder

Recipe

Read left to right. Merged cells mean ingredients are combined or processed together.

Fermented Orange Peels

Room-temperature brine — no hot syrup yet
peels from 4–5 organic oranges scrub thoroughly slice into 2–3 mm needle strips push peels under surface; stir 2–3×/day ferment RT 4–7 days (open lid or cloth) drain; reserve tonic
3 cups filtered water (RT) mix dissolve L. plantarum
3 Tbs. inulin powder
1/4 tsp. monk fruit powder
1/2 tsp. L. plantarum powder

Day 1–2: microbes wake up → Day 3–4: bitterness drops → Day 5–7: peels soften & turn tangy

Save the leftover liquid — you will turn it into the postbiotic syrup next. For more tang, scale inulin and L. plantarum up to 3× in the brine above.

Postbiotic Syrup

Keep under 140°F (60°C) when coating peels — hotter kills L. plantarum
reserved ferment tonic whisk until syrupy toss peels to coat under 140°F (60°C) air-dry 12–24h until gummy (no oven)
3 Tbs. inulin powder
1/8 tsp. monk fruit powder
drained fermented peels

Syrup must stay under 140°F (60°C) when mixed with the peels, or the live L. plantarum will die. Extra syrup is a tangy postbiotic tonic — sip it or keep it chilled. For more sweetness, scale syrup inulin and monk fruit up to 3×.

White Chocolate Enrobing

Keep under 95°F (35°C) — barely warm to the touch
120 g cacao butter melt gently under 95°F whisk dip syrup-glazed strips fridge 20 min to set
3 Tbs. inulin powder
1/8 tsp. monk fruit powder
syrup-glazed orange strips

Functional Benefits

Complete Tri-Biotic System

  • Prebiotic: Organic inulin paired with native citrus pectin nourishes gut flora
  • Probiotic: Live L. plantarum introduces active, gut-strengthening cultures
  • Postbiotic matrix: Fermented citrus metabolites plus cultured buttermilk peptides for immune and gut-barrier support

Immune Support

  • Uncooked orange rinds preserve heat-sensitive Vitamin C
  • Live L. plantarum supports the gut barrier, where much of the immune system resides

PMF & Polyphenol Absorption

  • Raw fermentation softens tough citrus fibers and unlocks dense antioxidants
  • Organic cacao butter acts as a lipid vehicle for hydrophobic citrus polymethoxyflavones (PMFs such as nobiletin)
  • Dark cacao flavonoids add complementary antioxidant support

Brain Health & Mouthfeel

  • Buttermilk phospholipids (MFGM) support cognitive nutrition while naturally emulsifying the sugar-free fats into a velvety texture

Zero-Glycemic Blood Sugar Control

  • Sweetened only with pure monk fruit and soluble prebiotic fiber
  • Avoids insulin spikes while satisfying sweet cravings

Synbiotic Acid-Shield Delivery

  • Cacao butter fats plus sticky inulin/pectin layers help encase live probiotics for safer transit through stomach acid toward the lower GI tract
