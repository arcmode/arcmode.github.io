Vegan · Transitioned grains · 12–16 h harvest · Cold lock

Check grains → 5-day dairy taper → 100% pea formula → cloth ferment 12–16 h → fridge lock

Background

Milk kefir grains are a dairy-adapted SCOBY — bacteria and yeasts bound in a rubbery polysaccharide matrix (kefiran). They can live on a plant base, but an overnight swap from cow’s milk to pea or nut milk often starves the bacteria, lets yeasts run, and shrinks the grains. This protocol tapers reconstituted dairy milk over five days while a pea-protein base plus a mineral buffer takes over.

The maintenance formula is built to keep alcohol down: less honey (yeast fuel), lemon juice as an initial acid shock, red marine algae as a multi-mineral stand-in for dairy, a breathable cover with daytime swirling so yeasts spend energy on cell walls instead of ethanol, an early 12–16 hour harvest, and an immediate fridge lock. These are milk kefir grains, not water kefir (tibicos).

Ingredients

Chlorine-free water. Stainless-steel mesh strainer and a wooden or stainless spoon — never rinse the grains. Lemon juice may be fresh-squeezed or preservative-free bottled.

Best practices

  • No plastic. Stainless-steel mesh strainer and a wooden or stainless spoon.
  • Never rinse the grains. Move them straight into the next batch.
  • Never a solid lid on vegan batches. A cloth or coffee filter keeps oxygen in play.
  • Swirl 2–3× during the vegan ferment so yeast builds cell walls instead of ethanol.
  • Harvest early (12–16 h), not at a 24-hour dairy cadence.
  • Cold-lock the drink as soon as it is strained.
  • Lemon juice may be fresh-squeezed or preservative-free bottled — same 1 teaspoon.
  • Wake-up cycle if grains were dehydrated or in transit more than 4 days: one 24-hour dairy ferment first.

Functional Benefits

Live kefir community on a plant base

  • SCOBY: milk-kefir bacteria and yeasts kept intact by a dairy taper instead of a hard cutover
  • Pea matrix: Folona pea milk powder (or undiluted soy milk) supplies protein the grains can work
  • Kefiran grains: the protocol is for grain growth, not a one-off drink

Low-alcohol controls

  • Less honey in the maintenance formula limits yeast fuel
  • Lemon acid shock at inoculation favors a clean tart start
  • Oxygen + swirling pushes yeast toward growth rather than ethanol
  • 12–16 h harvest and an immediate fridge lock cut the ferment short

Mineral buffer

  • Red marine algae stands in for dairy minerals (a multi-mineral buffer at 1/8 tsp per 500 mL)
  • Aimed at grain health on a plant substrate, not as a seaweed supplement shot

How to drink it

  • Strain, seal, refrigerate. Drink cold.
  • Next batch starts as soon as the grains come out — they should not sit dry
