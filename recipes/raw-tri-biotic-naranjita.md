# The Raw Tri-Biotic Naranjita

*100% Sugar-Free · Zero Glycemic · Raw Bio-Active Artisanal Treat*

Homemade orange-peel ferment: peels cultured with *L. plantarum*, inulin, and monk fruit → postbiotic syrup (ferment tonic + inulin/monk fruit) → sugar-free chocolate coat (white, dark, or milk).

## Background

Naranjitas are a classic Chilean candy: orange peels boiled soft, then cooked in sugar syrup until glossy and sweet. This version keeps the chew and bright citrus character, but skips the boil and the sugar — built for health benefits. Thin raw peels ferment with *L. plantarum*; the reserved tonic becomes a postbiotic syrup with inulin and monk fruit; then a sugar-free chocolate coat. The ferment softens the peels and leaves a pleasant tang that carries through to the finished candy.

Because we are not boiling, slice the peels paper-thin so the bacteria can penetrate the fibers and make them pleasant to chew.

## Recipe

Flowchart tables (Cooking For Engineers style) live on the [HTML page](raw-tri-biotic-naranjita.html). Method below mirrors those tables.

### 1. Slice and prepare (raw)

- Thoroughly scrub 4–5 organic oranges.
- Slice the peels into very thin, needle-like strips (about 2–3 mm wide) so they soften fully during fermentation without heat.

### 2. The raw brine ferment

Instead of a hot sugar syrup up front, create a room-temperature prebiotic bath to activate *L. plantarum*.

- In a glass jar, mix **3 cups** room-temperature filtered water, **3 Tbs.** inulin powder, and **1/4 tsp.** pure monk fruit powder.
- Stir in **1/2 tsp.** *L. plantarum* powder until fully dissolved.
- Add the thin raw orange strips. Push the peels under the surface as much as possible — no weight needed.
- Cover with an open lid or a cloth. Ferment at room temperature **4 to 7 days**, stirring **2–3 times per day** so the microbes don’t sink and stay at the bottom.
- Drain the peels and **reserve the tonic** for the syrup.

*Optional:* for more tang, scale brine inulin and *L. plantarum* up to **3×**.

`[Day 1–2: Microbes wake up] → [Day 3–4: Bitterness drops] → [Day 5–7: Peels soften & turn tangy]`

### 3. Postbiotic syrup

- Whisk **3 Tbs.** inulin and **1/8 tsp.** monk fruit into the reserved ferment tonic until syrupy.
- Toss the drained peels to coat. Keep the syrup **under 140°F (60°C)** when mixing with the peels — hotter temperatures kill *L. plantarum*.
- Lay the strips flat on parchment and air-dry **12 to 24 hours** until gummy. Do not use an oven.
- Extra syrup is a tangy postbiotic tonic — sip it or keep it chilled.

*Optional:* for more sweetness (especially after a tangier ferment), scale syrup inulin and monk fruit up to **3×**.

### 4. Chocolate enrobing — choose one

Keep cacao butter **under 95°F (35°C)** — barely warm to the touch. Dip the syrup-glazed strips, then refrigerate **20 minutes** to set.

#### White chocolate

- Melt **120 g** cacao butter.
- Whisk in **3 Tbs.** inulin and **1/8 tsp.** monk fruit.
- Dip strips; fridge 20 min to set.

#### Dark chocolate

- Melt **120 g** cacao butter.
- Whisk in **3 Tbs.** cacao powder, **3 Tbs.** inulin, and **1/8 tsp.** monk fruit.
- Dip strips; fridge 20 min to set.

#### Milk chocolate

- Melt **120 g** cacao butter.
- Whisk in **2 Tbs.** cacao powder, **2 Tbs.** cultured buttermilk powder, **3 Tbs.** inulin, and **1/8 tsp.** monk fruit.
- Dip strips; fridge 20 min to set.

## Functional Benefits

### Complete Tri-Biotic System
- **Prebiotic:** Organic inulin paired with native citrus pectin nourishes gut flora
- **Probiotic:** Live *L. plantarum* introduces active, gut-strengthening cultures
- **Postbiotic Matrix:** Fermented citrus metabolites plus cultured buttermilk peptides for immune and gut-barrier support

### Immune Support
- Uncooked orange rinds preserve heat-sensitive Vitamin C
- Live *L. plantarum* supports the gut barrier, where much of the immune system resides

### PMF & Polyphenol Absorption
- Raw fermentation softens tough citrus fibers and unlocks dense antioxidants
- Organic cacao butter acts as a lipid vehicle for hydrophobic citrus polymethoxyflavones (PMFs such as nobiletin)
- Dark cacao flavonoids add complementary antioxidant support

### Brain Health & Mouthfeel
- Buttermilk phospholipids (MFGM) support cognitive nutrition while naturally emulsifying the sugar-free fats into a velvety texture

### Zero-Glycemic Blood Sugar Control
- Sweetened only with pure monk fruit and soluble prebiotic fiber
- Avoids insulin spikes while satisfying sweet cravings

### Synbiotic Acid-Shield Delivery
- Cacao butter fats plus sticky inulin/pectin layers help encase live probiotics for safer transit through stomach acid toward the lower GI tract
